Loft conversion timeline in Worthing: survey to sign-off

Key facts

  • Eight stages: enquiry, survey, design and quote, planning if needed, Building Regulations and party wall, build, fit-out, sign-off.
  • Build time ranges from 4–6 weeks (Velux) to 12–16 weeks (mansard).
  • Planning and Building Regulations work usually runs in parallel.

Think of the journey as three tracks that meet at the building stage: permission, regulation and neighbours.

What is the order?

Enquiry, survey, design and quote, planning permission where needed, Building Regulations and party wall notices, the build, fit-out, then final Building Control sign-off. Each stage has its own realistic timeframe.

StageWhat happens
1 EnquiryFirst read on suitability
2 SurveyHead height, roof structure, staircase options
3 Design and quoteLayout, type, specification, firm quote
4 PlanningPermitted development check or full application
5 Regulations and party wallBuilding Control application; notices to neighbours
6 BuildStructure, roof, insulation, stairs, first fix
7 Fit-outPlastering, flooring, decoration, bathroom fittings
8 Sign-offFinal inspection and completion certificate

What runs at the same time?

A full planning application, where needed, runs alongside finalising Building Regulations drawings, so it does not necessarily double the delay. Party wall notice should go out in good time before work on the shared wall.

How long does the build take?

Around 4–6 weeks for a Velux, 8–10 for a standard dormer, 10–14 for an L-shaped dormer or hip-to-gable, and 12–16 for a mansard, excluding design and planning time beforehand.

What if the survey says no?

The survey is what confirms what is achievable. Skipping it risks a design that does not match the real roof structure.
