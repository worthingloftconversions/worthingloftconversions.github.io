Planning permission for a loft conversion in Adur and Worthing

Key facts

  • Most mansard conversions need a full planning application.
  • Householder application £548; proposed-use Lawful Development Certificate £274 (from 1 April 2026).
  • Optional council advice: £75 inc. VAT ("do I need planning permission?") or £150 inc. VAT (pre-application).
  • A full application adds several weeks to months before building starts.

This page is for the moment permitted development says no, or you want certainty before you spend on drawings.

When do I need a planning application?

When the design exceeds the permitted development limits, when your home is in a conservation area or under an Article 4 Direction, or when a previous planning condition removed the right. Most mansard conversions fall in this group because they rebuild most of the rear roof.

Who do I apply to?

Adur & Worthing Councils' planning service, unless the property is inside the South Downs National Park, where the National Park Authority is the planning authority. Properties near Worthing's northern edge should check which side of the boundary they are on.

What does it cost?

A full householder planning application currently costs £548, and a proposed-use Lawful Development Certificate £274, both effective 1 April 2026 and subject to change. The council also offers optional written advice at £75 inc. VAT and a desktop pre-application assessment at £150 inc. VAT.

How long does it take?

Timescales vary with application type and council workload, so confirm current target decision periods with the planning service. Plan for the application to add several weeks to months before construction can start.

Does it run alongside Building Regulations?

Yes. The two approvals cover different things and typically run in parallel. Planning approval does not mean Building Regulations approval, or the other way round. See building regulations.
